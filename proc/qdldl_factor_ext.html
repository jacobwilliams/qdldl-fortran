qdldl_factor_ext

public function qdldl_factor_ext(n, Ap, Ai, Ax, Lp, Li, Lx, D, Dinv, Lnz, etree, bwork, iwork, fwork, zero_tol, regularize, reg_eps, reg_delta, n_positive, n_negative, n_zero, n_regularized, zero_col, signs) result(status)

qdldl_factor with a zero-pivot tolerance, dynamic regularization, and the inertia of D.

Each pivot is classified, as computed (before any replacement), as zero ( zero_tol), positive, or negative. Then:

  • Without regularization, a zero pivot stops the factorization (qdldl_error_zero_pivot, with its column in zero_col), as upstream does (with zero_tol = 0, only an exact zero).
  • With regularization, a pivot with reg_eps, or a zero pivot, is replaced by reg_delta, where is the expected sign of the pivot: signs(k) if it is given and nonzero, else the sign of itself ( for zero). The factorization then always completes; n_regularized counts the replacements.
