The public interface of the QDLDL library: qdldl_type, a sparse solver for quasi-definite matrices, with a fill-reducing ordering, and the low-level routines of qdldl_core.

Typical use:

  type(qdldl_type) :: ldl
  call ldl%analyze(n, irow, icol, istat)   ! the pattern, once
  call ldl%factor(val, istat)              ! values in the pattern's order
  call ldl%solve(b, istat)                 ! b is overwritten by x

The factorization does not pivot. It is stable for quasi-definite matrices with and positive definite, in any symmetric order. For other matrices it may run, but can be unstable; see the regularization options of qdldl_type.

private subroutine analyze_csc(me, n, Ap, Ai, istat, perm, ordering)

analyze for a matrix given in compressed sparse column (CSC) form: the entries of column j are Ai(Ap(j):Ap(j+1)-1), with Ap(1) = 1. Normally the upper triangle (as for the low-level routines), but, as for coordinate input, entries of either triangle are accepted and duplicates are added. The values given to factor are then in the order of Ai.

private subroutine set_signs(me, signs, istat)

Sets the expected sign of each pivot (in the original order of the rows of ), for regularization: +1 for a row of the positive definite block (), -1 for one of the negative definite block (), 0 if unknown (the sign is then inferred from the values). private subroutine solve(me, b, istat, refine)

Solves in place with the factorization, with optional iterative refinement against the (unregularized) matrix: steps of , , while the scaled residual is above round-off and each step at least halves the residual (a step that doesn't reduce it is discarded). Allocates nothing.
